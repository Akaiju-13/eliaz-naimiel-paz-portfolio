  1. 01 / INGEST

    Raw footage in.

    Flat, ungraded and untrimmed. First job is sorting takes, syncing audio and marking the moments worth keeping.

  2. 02 / CUT

    Cut to the hook.

    The first three seconds decide everything, so the strongest moment goes first and every pause gets trimmed out.

  3. 03 / CAPTIONS

    Words that pop.

    Most feeds play on mute. Captions land word by word, with the key phrase highlighted so it reads at a glance.

  4. 04 / COLOR

    Grade it like a brand.

    Shots get matched, skin tones protected and one consistent look applied across the whole batch.

  5. 05 / SOUND

    Mix the punch.

    Dialogue cleaned and leveled, music bed under it, and SFX on every transition so the cuts hit.

  6. 06 / EXPORT

    Ship to every feed.

    Exported per platform at the right ratio, size and safe zones, with hook variations ready for testing.
